# Experience System

Anonymized practitioner material — cleaned, classified, structured.

**Status:** empty, awaiting material. The ingestion pipeline is defined
(MASTER_PROMPT §31); no records are published until real material arrives —
nothing is invented to fill the space.

- **Notes** — short useful experiences (100–400 words)
- **Reports** — substantial individual experiences (400–1500+ words)
- **Patterns** — recurring phenomena across independent reports
- **Questions** — real practitioner questions

## Privacy rules

Real names, usernames, phones, emails, locations and private identifiers are
removed before publication. Direct quotes require explicit permission;
otherwise material is paraphrased and anonymized.
