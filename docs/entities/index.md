## Cross-collection entities

Where a term has both a concept page and a glossary entry (dao, de, qi, zuowang, shouyi, xinzhai, wuwei, jingzuo, xing, ming, yi, tiaoxi, neiguan, daoyin, qigong, neigong),
the two pages share one graph entity under the bare slug.
